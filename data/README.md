# Data Fixtures & Fictional Data Declaration

All personal identities, customer account records, phone numbers, email addresses, and specific store inventory records contained within this directory are **entirely fictional and invented** for demonstration, evaluation, and educational purposes.

- **Customer Profiles (`customer_profiles.json`)**: All customer names, IDs (`CUST-1001` through `CUST-1100`), phone numbers (`555-01XX`), and email addresses (`*@example.com`) are synthetic fixtures generated for testing loyalty tier logic.
- **Sales Transactions (`Coffee Shop Sales.xlsx`)**: Transaction ledger records representing mock coffee shop purchase orders across store IDs 3, 5, and 8.
- **Raw Materials & Recipes (`raw_materials_costs.csv`, `product_recipes.csv`)**: Invented bill-of-materials and ingredient unit costs used to demonstrate Cost-of-Goods-Sold (COGS) and gross margin calculations.
- **Store Inventory (`store_inventory.json`)**: Mock stock balance levels across simulated store locations.

No real-world personally identifiable information (PII), customer data, or proprietary commercial secrets are present.
